# Phase 8: Interview Preparation & Execution

**Difficulty:** Advanced | **Estimated Duration:** 3–4 Weeks | **Topics:** 4

> **Overview:** Transforms raw technical knowledge from Phases 1–7 into interview performance: pattern recognition under time pressure, structured communication, behavioral leadership stories, resume impact, and timed mock execution.

## Why This Phase Is Ordered Here
Placed as the capstone phase so candidates synthesize their coding, LLD, HLD, and production engineering skills into timed, high-signal interview delivery.

## Topics in This Phase

### 8.1 Coding Interview Strategy & 15 Core DSA Practice Patterns
- **Tier:** `Must Learn (P0)` | **Difficulty:** `Advanced` | **Est. Study Time:** `20 hrs`
- **What It Covers:** Maps coding prompts to the 15 recurring DSA patterns (Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Fast/Slow Pointers, Merge Intervals, Cyclic Sort, Monotonic Stack, Top-K Heap, Tree BFS/DFS, Graph Topo Sort/Union-Find, Binary Search on Answer, Backtracking, and 1D/2D DP).
- **Why It Is Important:** In a 45-minute coding round, recognizing the underlying pattern in 2–3 minutes leaves 35+ minutes for clean implementation, dry-running, and complexity analysis.
- **Completion Criteria:** Can solve Medium DSA problems in <25 minutes while articulating invariants, edge cases, and exact complexity.

- [ ] Pattern recognition matrix: mapping problem constraints (N <= 20 vs. N <= 10^5) to target time complexity — `P0`
- [ ] 45-Minute Coding Protocol: Clarify constraints (3m) -> Approach & complexity (5m) -> Clean code (25m) -> Dry run & edge cases (7m) — `P0`
- [ ] Curated 75 / 150 problem sheet mastery across all 15 patterns without hints — `P0`

### 8.2 Technical Communication, Mock Interviews & Design Rubrics
- **Tier:** `Must Learn (P0)` | **Difficulty:** `Advanced` | **Est. Study Time:** `10 hrs`
- **What It Covers:** Covers how interviewers score rubrics across Coding, LLD, and HLD rounds, how to drive trade-off discussions, handle hints gracefully, and apply the repository's 6-point Interview Readiness Assessment.
- **Why It Is Important:** Many strong engineers fail interviews due to silent coding, jumping into implementation before clarifying scope, or failing to defend trade-offs.
- **Completion Criteria:** Achieves Level 3+ ('Explain and apply unaided') across all Must-Learn (P0) topics in the Readiness Assessment.

- [ ] Repository 6-Point Interview Readiness Self-Assessment (0–4 scale: aim for 3+ on all P0 topics) — `P0`
- [ ] Driving LLD & HLD interviews: pacing the 45-minute clock, proposing trade-offs proactively, and checking in with the interviewer — `P0`
- [ ] Mock interview feedback loop: recording and auditing communication clarity, hint reception, and bug discovery — `P1`

### 8.3 Behavioral Interviews (STAR Method), Leadership & Resume Prep
- **Tier:** `Must Learn (P0)` | **Difficulty:** `Intermediate` | **Est. Study Time:** `8 hrs`
- **What It Covers:** Covers engineering resume optimization (impact-first XYZ bullets), building a 6–8 story matrix using the STAR framework (Situation, Task, Action, Result), conflict resolution, failure/postmortem stories, and company-specific values.
- **Why It Is Important:** Behavioral and leadership rounds carry veto power at top tech companies and often decide between adjacent levels (e.g., L4 vs. L5).
- **Completion Criteria:** Has a polished 1-page resume and 6 rehearsed STAR stories adaptable to any behavioral prompt.

- [ ] Engineering Resume optimization: Action + Metric + Technical Context (XYZ formula) and ATS clarity — `P0`
- [ ] STAR Story Matrix: Ownership, Technical Disagreement, Tight Deadline, Production Incident/Failure, Mentorship, Ambiguity — `P0`
- [ ] Company-specific preparation: Amazon Leadership Principles, Google Googleyness, Meta Engineering Execution — `P1`

### 8.4 Structured Interview Revision Plans (4-Week, 8-Week & 12-Week)
- **Tier:** `Should Learn (P1)` | **Difficulty:** `Intermediate` | **Est. Study Time:** `6 hrs`
- **What It Covers:** Provides actionable week-by-week revision schedules (4-week sprint for experienced engineers, 8-week balanced plan, and 12-week beginner-to-advanced plan) plus final-week cheatsheets.
- **Why It Is Important:** Spaced repetition prevents forgetting Phase 2 DSA patterns while studying Phase 6–7 System Design and Cloud topics.
- **Completion Criteria:** Completed a full revision cycle across all P0 topics with >85% readiness score.

- [ ] 12-Week Comprehensive Track (Beginner to SDE-1/SDE-2) — `P0`
- [ ] 8-Week Accelerated Track (Intermediate SDE Refresher) — `P1`
- [ ] 4-Week High-Yield Sprint (P0 Must-Learn Topics + Daily Timed Drills) — `P1`
